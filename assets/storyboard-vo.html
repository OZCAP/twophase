Notes for the editor

Voice first

  • The graphics illustrate the line; they don't repeat it. Keep on-screen words to labels (“REALLY”, “go nuts!”, button names).
  • Cut on the stressed word in each line, e.g. the second REALLY, Twophase, adventurous.

One visual metaphor

  • Queries are capsules on a wire. Green ones (reads) pass, orange ones (changes) wait at the Twophase logo.
  • Same logo, same colours in every shot, so the end logo feels earned.

Callbacks

  • The 🍹 from the emoji flick in shot 05 comes back in shot 13. That's the whole joke, so use the same emoji at the same size.
  • The “10 minutes” in the VO matches the app's real “Trust 10 min” button.

Sound

  • Light, playful bed. Drop it out for the “REALLY” punch and the phone buzz in shot 13.
  • A “thunk” on the logo drop, a soft chime on approvals, a resolve on the logo.
