Twophase: logo options

Four directions. Each one tells the same story, the agent prepares, you commit, in a different way.

Option A · Database-native

Prepared → Committed

A database cylinder in two layers. The top layer is dashed: a change that's prepared but not yet real. The solid base is what's committed. It reads instantly as “database”, and the dashed layer is the human step.

Option B · Monogram

2✓

The numeral 2 whose base stroke becomes a check mark. It reads as the name and the action at once: two phases, ending in your approval. The green tail ties it to the Approve button.

Option C · Minimal

Split check

A check mark drawn in two separate strokes. The short amber stroke is the agent preparing, the gap is the pause, and the long white stroke is you committing. It's the simplest of the four and holds up best at tiny sizes.

Option D · Abstract

Phase waves

Two waves a quarter-cycle apart, literally two phases. The dashed wave above is the proposal, and the solid one below is the decision that follows it. It keeps the current red so it feels like an evolution, not a restart.
